LifePilot brings the moving parts of a student day into one clear, calm view.

Start with a briefing that combines lectures, deadlines, messages, travel updates and priorities. Review prepared actions, see why each one matters, and decide what happens next. Nothing is approved silently.

Built first for UK computing students and designed to grow for wider use, LifePilot includes:

• A daily readiness briefing
• A connected timeline for events, messages, tasks and travel
• Smart approval cards with plain-language reasoning
• Schedule import from screenshots using on-device text recognition
• An iOS Share extension for adding calendar invitations from Mail, Gmail or the web
• A Home Screen widget for the next event
• Live Activities and Dynamic Island updates on supported iPhone models
• Visible, correctable memory and personal insights
• Light, dark and system appearance modes
• Local profile, privacy and approval controls

This TechFest edition is an interactive on-device showcase. It uses realistic sample data for connected sources and does not sign in to, read from or change real email, calendar, travel or finance accounts. Selected schedule screenshots are processed on device. The app does not collect or track personal data.

LifePilot prepares. You decide.
