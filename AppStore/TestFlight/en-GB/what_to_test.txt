Please test the complete first-run flow and the following paths:

1. Finish onboarding and review the Home briefing.
2. Open a prepared action, approve or dismiss it, relaunch the app and confirm the decision persists.
3. In Timeline, import a schedule screenshot and verify the extracted title, date, time and location before saving.
4. Share a calendar invitation to LifePilot from Mail, Gmail or Safari, edit the event if needed and confirm it appears in the schedule.
5. Add the LifePilot widget and confirm it shows the next event.
6. In Settings, start and end a Live Activity, switch appearance, edit the local profile and review Data & Privacy.

No sign-in is required. Connected sources intentionally use local sample data in this TechFest build.
