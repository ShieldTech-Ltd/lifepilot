# LifePilot Privacy Policy

Effective: 1 August 2026

LifePilot is a student-first planning application. This policy describes the
TechFest showcase build and will be updated before any future release changes
how information is handled.

## Information Stored on Your Device

LifePilot stores the following information locally:

- Profile details you enter, including name, email, course, university,
  location, briefing time, and an optional profile photo.
- Preferences such as appearance, enabled demo sources, and alert settings.
- Approval history and imported calendar events.
- Onboarding and demonstration state.

The LifePilot app, widget, and Share extension use a private Apple App Group to
share the next calendar event, approval summary, and imported events. Other apps
cannot access this App Group.

## Photos and Schedule Screenshots

LifePilot can access only photos you explicitly select with Apple's photo
picker. A selected profile photo is stored locally until you remove it, reset
the app, or uninstall LifePilot.

Schedule screenshots are processed on device using Apple's text-recognition
technology. LifePilot stores the event details you confirm, but does not retain
the source screenshot.

## Data Collection, Tracking, and Third Parties

The TechFest showcase build does not transmit personal information to
LifePilot, analytics services, advertising networks, AI providers, or other
third parties. It does not track activity across apps or websites. Connected
sources use demonstration data and do not access real email, calendar, travel,
or financial accounts.

## Retention and Deletion

Local information remains on the device until you remove it, choose **Reset
local demo data** in LifePilot Settings, or uninstall the app. Resetting clears
profile edits, approval history, imported events, source settings, appearance,
and onboarding state.

## Your Choices

You can:

- Pause any connected demonstration source.
- Remove a selected profile photo.
- Approve or dismiss every prepared action.
- Reset all local LifePilot data from Settings.
- Uninstall LifePilot to remove its local application data.
