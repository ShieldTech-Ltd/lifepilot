Common questions

Why do connected sources show sample information?
The TechFest edition is an on-device showcase. Calendar, email, travel and finance sources use realistic sample data and do not connect to real accounts.

How do I import a schedule?
Open Timeline, choose Add schedule from screenshot, select an image and review every extracted field before saving.

How do I add an invitation?
Open a calendar invitation in Mail, Gmail or Safari, choose Share, select LifePilot and review the event before adding it.

How do I remove local data?
Open Settings, then Data & Privacy, then Reset local demo data. Uninstalling LifePilot also removes its local app data.
