LifePilot Privacy Policy

Effective 1 August 2026

LifePilot is a student-first planning application. This policy describes the TechFest showcase build and will be updated before a future release changes how information is handled.

Information stored on your device

LifePilot stores profile details you enter, preferences, approval history, imported calendar events, and demonstration state locally. The app, widget, and Share extension use a private Apple App Group to share upcoming-event and approval information.

Photos and schedule screenshots

LifePilot can access only photos you explicitly select with Apple's photo picker. A profile photo remains local until you remove it, reset the app, or uninstall LifePilot. Schedule screenshots are processed on device with Apple's text recognition. The event details you confirm are stored, but the source screenshot is not retained by LifePilot.

Collection, tracking, and third parties

The TechFest showcase build does not transmit personal information to LifePilot, analytics services, advertising networks, AI providers, or other third parties. It does not track activity across apps or websites. Connected sources use demonstration data and do not access real accounts.

Retention and deletion

Local information remains until you remove it, choose Reset local demo data in Settings, or uninstall LifePilot. Resetting clears profile edits, approval history, imported events, source settings, appearance, and onboarding state.

Your choices

  • Pause any connected demonstration source.
  • Remove a selected profile photo.
  • Approve or dismiss every prepared action.
  • Reset all local LifePilot data from Settings.
  • Uninstall LifePilot to remove its local application data.
